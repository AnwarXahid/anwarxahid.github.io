**Today's AI** ([todaysai.ai](https://todaysai.ai/)) is a discovery-and-learning
platform for artificial-intelligence tools. It pairs a searchable, categorized
directory of AI services with an *AI learning* section and a built-in assistant
that answers questions about models, tools, and how to apply them.

I designed and built the system as Creator &amp; Lead Developer under the direction
of <a href="https://weile.work/" rel="noopener">Prof. Wei Le</a>. The goal is
practical AI literacy: giving K-12 students, undergraduates, graduate students,
and self-directed learners a low-friction way to find a tool that fits their task
and to understand what it actually does.
